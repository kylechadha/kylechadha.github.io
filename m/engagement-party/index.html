Help-yourself drink station

Kyle owns drinks + cooler
The idea

One non-alcoholic mixer in the dispenser.

Keep it cold, bright and good on its own. Put mezcal and whiskey beside it so people can customize their own cup. Kyle's call on the final mixer — blood orange + ginger, grapefruit/citrus, apple + ginger, or another simple spritz.

Cooler

Keep it easy

Refill plan

  • Start dispenser ~¾ full.
  • Keep extra still base cold in insulated bottles/pitchers.
  • Add fizzy ingredients in smaller batches so they stay bubbly.
  • Keep ice in cups, not in the dispenser, to avoid dilution.

Cameras & keepsakes

Let friends capture it

No formal photo activity required
Camp Snap CS8

Video messages + little moments

We'll leave the camera on and ready to use.

  1. Pick it up and frame your shot through the viewfinder.
  2. Press and hold the trigger/button to record.
  3. Look for the red recording light in the viewfinder so you know it's recording.
  4. Let go when you're done. Each recording saves as its own clip.
Card copy:
Leave us a little video 💛 Hold the button down and make sure you see the red light in the viewfinder. Say hi, give us advice, tell a story, or film whatever's happening. Let go when you're done.
Photos

Screen-free snapshots

Set out the Camp Snap photo camera and/or disposable cameras where people can grab them naturally. No reviewing, no deleting, no instructions beyond point + shoot.

  • Put them somewhere visible but not precious.
  • One small sign is enough: “Take some pics for us.”
  • Collect all cameras before cleanup.
