Cachuma runs warm by day and cold at night (often a ~30°F swing). Bring layers and a warm sleeping bag.

🔥 Two heads-ups

  • Fire ring: our water/electric sites may only have a charcoal BBQ grill, not a wood fire ring. If that's true (worth a quick call to the park to check), we could buy or bring a portable fire pit. Firewood's sold at the camp store. Switching to a standard fire-ring site is an option only if any are still open — checking that.
  • No swimming in the lake: it's a drinking reservoir, so no body contact for people or dogs. Dogs can ride in the kayak, though. For a dog swim, Red Rock on the Santa Ynez River (~25–30 min) is the spot.

🚣 Activities

Solvang

Danish village ~15 min away — wine tasting, breweries, and bakeries. The easy chill-day pick if people want a break from camp.

Kayaking & boating

Rent at the marina — kayaks (1- & 2-person), canoes, pontoons. Dogs allowed aboard, so they can ride along. First-come.

~$15/hr or $50/day · (805) 688-4040

Fishing

One of SoCal's best — trout, bass, crappie, catfish, and the lake's near full right now. License required 16+, buy at the marina.

Disc golf

18-hole course on-site, starts/ends at the store. Beginner-friendly — bring discs (park entry applies).

Mountain biking

No rentals at the lake. Bike shops in Buellton/Solvang (~15 min) and Santa Barbara rent bikes — call ahead to confirm mountain bikes. Ride Tequepis/Snyder or the campground roads.

Red Rock (dog swim)

Since the lake is no-swim, Red Rock on the Santa Ynez River (~25–30 min) has deep, dog-friendly pools — the spot to let the dogs swim.

🎒 Packing essentials

  • Warm layers + sleeping bag (cold nights)
  • Mesh food covers (yellowjackets midday)
  • Sunscreen + bug spray
  • Camp stove + fuel
  • Dogs: food, leash (≤6 ft), bed, water bowl, waste bags, rabies vaccination proof
