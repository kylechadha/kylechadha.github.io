Los Danzantes Michelin ★ Green Star Top Pick
Fri 9:00–10:00 PM
Modern Oaxacan · Sustainability-focused · A la carte · Own mezcal line

Levadura de Olla Michelin ★ Top Pick
Sat 7:00–8:00 PM
Traditional Oaxacan · Heritage / Pre-Columbian · A la carte

Chef Thalia Barrios Garcia's deeply personal tribute to her hometown of San Mateo Yucutindoo in the Sierra Sur highlands. Heritage pre-Columbian recipes from small villages you literally cannot get anywhere else. Vegetable-forward with two paths: traditional regional and creative-modern. The non-alcoholic drinks (agua de maíz, pozontle) are stars in their own right.

Selva #29 Best Bars North America
Cocktail bar · Upscale design · Upstairs from Los Danzantes

Mezcaloteca
Tasting room · Guided educational experience · Reservation required · No cocktails

Not a bar — a mezcal library. Founded in 2010, this is the OG tasting room of Oaxaca. Dark, moody speakeasy vibes. Your host guides you through 3–5 small-batch mezcals from family producers you won't find outside Mexico. Sipped from traditional gourd cups. More intellectual than romantic — best as a late-afternoon primer before dinner.

In Situ
Tasting room · 180+ bottles · Walk-in · Neat mezcal only, no cocktails

The cathedral of mezcal. Walls lined floor-to-ceiling with 180+ bottles from the collection of founders Ulises Torrentera (mezcal author) and Sandra Ortiz Brena. If you want to taste mezcals you've never heard of — wild agave Jabali, ancestral clay-pot-distilled Tepextate — this is the place. No pretension, just deep knowledge.

What We Can Eat

Our restrictions work well with Oaxacan cuisine

Oaxacan cuisine is naturally on our side — pork, chicken, lamb, fish, and mole dominate, so skipping beef is never a problem. Shrimp and fish are the primary seafood, so no lobster/crab is equally easy. Levadura de Olla is the most accommodating (vegetable-forward, explicitly dietary-friendly). Los Danzantes handles restrictions professionally with a la carte flexibility. For tasting menus, just mention our restrictions when booking and they'll adjust.
